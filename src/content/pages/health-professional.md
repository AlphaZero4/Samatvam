## Continuing Medical Education:

Project DEEPA is directed towards Diabetes physicians, counsellors and other health professionals. This fosters the much needed Diabetes Health Care Manpower Development and Capacity Building, through local, national and international: Scholarships - Observerships - Internships and Fellowships; Continuing medical education and training
